Step 1 of 7 · Mission

Mission: Hear the other side of the story.

RebuttaLDS offers a calm, well-sourced counter-argument to the polished salesmanship of Latter-day Saint proselytizers—so people can consider a consequential invitation with fuller context.

Context before commitment
Why this exists

Persuasion is easier to recognize when you can see the playbook.

Missionaries are trained to listen for a person’s grievance, uncertainty, or unmet need; present the LDS narrative as an emotionally resonant solution; and guide the conversation toward belief and commitment.

Most everyday citizens have not been prepared for that refined persuasive approach. RebuttaLDS helps restore balance by placing the counterpoints, historical context, and questions they may not otherwise hear within reach.

Our purposeNot hostility toward individual missionaries or believers, but informed choice: a clear opportunity to weigh the other side before making a decision.
